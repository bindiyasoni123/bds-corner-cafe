Our little corner

A proper home-cooked welcome

Blondie's Diner sits on the side of The Hardwick on Blackhall front street — a warm, friendly spot where the kettle's always on and the breakfasts are the kind that set you up for the day.

Everything is made in the shop, from hearty full breakfasts and jacket potatoes to our rotisserie chicken, homemade pies and a counter full of cakes. Whether you're calling in after a walk down to Blackhall Rocks, grabbing a takeaway on the way to work, or settling in for Sunday lunch with the family, there's a seat and a good feed waiting for you.

We look after every plate — including gluten-free, vegetarian and vegan needs — so just ask and we'll sort you out.
